---
title: Write news
description: How EELSdb moderators write, publish and edit news articles on the website.
---

Moderators write the news on the website. Open **News** in the staff menu, or go to the [news page](/news/) and choose **Write or edit news**.
Like all moderation pages, it needs [two-step sign-in](/docs/moderate/review/#two-step-sign-in-is-required).

## Write an article

1. Choose **Write an article**.
2. Give it a title and write the article. The editor shows the article as readers will see it.
3. Write a summary of one or two sentences. It shows in the news list and when someone shares the link.
4. Check the authors. You are the first author. Search for people with an EELSdb account, or type any name to credit someone without one. The byline lists the authors in this order.
5. Check the web address. EELSdb makes it from the title. You can change it until you publish.
6. Choose **Save draft** to finish later, or **Publish** to put the article on the news page.

Only moderators can see a draft. Use **Preview** at the top of the editor to see it as a page.

## Formatting

The toolbar has the formatting that articles use:

- Text style: paragraph, heading or subheading.
- Bold, italic, superscript (x²), subscript (x₂) and links.
- Bulleted lists, numbered lists and quotes.
- Images. Drag them in, paste them, or use the image button. EELSdb shrinks each image to 1600 pixels wide. Click an image to describe it for people who cannot see it, and to set its size and position.
- YouTube videos. Use the video button, or paste a YouTube link on its own. Click a video to change its link, size or position. Readers see a picture of the video, and the player loads only when they press play.
- Link cards: a box that links to a page, with a title and an optional short description. Use the link card button, and click a card to change it.
- Note boxes: note, tip, caution or danger. Click inside a box to change its style.
- Dividers.
- Equations, written in LaTeX, for example `t/\lambda = \ln(I_t/I_0)`. They can sit in the text or on their own line. Click an equation to change it.

**Markdown** at the right of the toolbar shows the article as Markdown, if you prefer to type it. **Visual** goes back.

## Edit a published article

Open the article from **News** and change it. **Save changes** updates the page on the site at once. A published article has no draft, and its web address no longer changes, so links to it keep working.

Each save keeps the version it replaced. To undo a change, choose **Restore** next to an earlier version at the bottom of the editor.

The news archive from the old website can be edited the same way. Restore the archived version to undo the edits.
